====================================================
FOUNDER BIOGRAPHY
====================================================

Aadhira Suleim A. R. is the Founder of ILMA – Biomedical Future, an educational platform dedicated exclusively to Biomedical Engineering students.

She completed her Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Biomedical Engineering from Rohini College of Engineering and Technology, Anna University, and is currently pursuing a Master of Engineering (M.E.) in Biomedical Engineering at Udaya School of Engineering, Anna University.

She founded ILMA with the vision of making Biomedical Engineering education more structured, accessible, and career-focused by providing career guidance, biomedical domains, skill roadmaps, government exam preparation, and learning resources.

Her interests include Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare, Medical Devices, Wearable Healthcare Technology, Biomedical Signal Processing, Medical Imaging, Embedded Systems, and Biomedical Research.
